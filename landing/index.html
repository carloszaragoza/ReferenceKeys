The German Concert case

One preset, nine renders, one mistake caught by the numbers.

L
R
  1. 01

    Version 1 sounded dark

    Built by ear from a description. Measured against the Roland, it had 12 dB too much deep bass and its brightness center sat at 220 Hz instead of 477.

  2. 02

    Nine renders later

    Same MIDI, rendered through every variant. Lo Boost off, the high shelf moved from 18 kHz to 8 kHz. The curve landed on the reference.

  3. 03

    Then it leaned left

    The stereo control I used was actually a balance knob. The width number looked perfect, but the piano sat 7 dB to the left. I heard it on the first play.

    Headphones make the 7 dB obvious.
  4. 04

    Fixed, then shipped

    The real width control, measured again: centered within 0.06 dB and the same width as the Roland.

How a preset gets calibrated

Measure the player

Four rounds of pp to ff on the MX88 set the velocity curve, so soft really means soft.

Render the same take

MIDI goes through the Keyscape plugin offline. Every render is bit-identical, so any change you hear is the patch.

Compare against the reference

Ten octave bands, tilt, stereo width and left and right balance.

Tune, then check again

Nine render passes for German Concert V2. One of them caught a 7 dB lean to the left before it shipped.
