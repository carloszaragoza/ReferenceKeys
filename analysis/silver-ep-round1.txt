Open
  31 Hz band: the demos hold almost nothing below 44 Hz (-37 dB); every candidate measured +24 to +32 there. Part is
  the test performance (F1/G1 detections that may be false), part is real sub content the Lo Cut and bass shelf
  do not reach. Not audible on most systems; check on headphones or a sub.
  Dynamics: not calibrated. Synthetic timing makes the dynamic range (9 to 13 dB) incomparable with the demos' 5.8 dB.
  The Tape Slammer P2 had no measurable effect. Check by playing.
  Untested knobs: Vintage 2-Band EQ P1 and P6 changed nothing measurable.

Format fix (2026-11-13): patches read straight out of the factory library database use an older internal format
(SYNTHENG Vers 22, no per-model interface data), so Keyscape showed the patch name but not the model. The patch
is now round-tripped through the Keyscape AU (tools/render/dumpstate) and saved in the format Keyscape writes
itself (Vers 29). Same calibrated values, identical render.

Model view fix (2026-11-13): the Vers 29 round-trip was not enough. Keyscape picks the model view (the MKS-20
panel and its controls) from SYNTHENG origLibName and origPatchName, which it writes only when a patch is saved
from its own browser. The factory patch was saved from Keyscape (Save Patch As) and the calibration applied on top:
same knob changes plus the panel mirrors (Custom_3 Bass, Custom_4 Treble, Custom_16 Chorus Depth, MOD_MATRIX defV2).
That save also carries the player's MX88 Wide velocity curve, which plays brighter, so Treble went 0.28 -> 0.14:
